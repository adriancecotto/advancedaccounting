Business Combination Case Study: Apex Holdings Inc. & Beacon Technologies Co.

1. Transaction Overview

On January 1, 2026, Apex Holdings Inc. acquired 100% of the outstanding common stock of Beacon Technologies Co. for $1,040,000 in cash. At the acquisition date, Beacon's stockholders' equity on its historical books was $600,000, consisting of:

  • Common Stock: $200,000
  • Additional Paid-in Capital: $20,000
  • Retained Earnings: $380,000

2. Identifiable Net Assets & Fair Value Adjustments

An independent valuation of Beacon Technologies Co.'s assets and liabilities revealed that book values equaled fair values except for the following items:

  • Land: Book value $70,000 | Fair value $150,000 (↑ +$80,000 excess)
  • Buildings: Book value $200,000 | Fair value $330,000 (↑ +$130,000 excess)
  • Equipment: Book value $150,000 | Fair value $120,000 (↓ -$30,000 deficit)
  • Unrecorded Brand Name: Book value $0 | Fair value $160,000 (↑ +$160,000 new asset)

3. Purchase Price Allocation & Goodwill Calculation

The total excess of purchase price over subsidiary book value is calculated as follows:

  • Purchase Price Paid: $1,040,000
  • Less: Subsidiary Book Value of Net Assets: ($600,000)
  • Total Excess over Book Value: $440,000
  • Allocated to Net Asset Fair Value Adjustments: +$340,000
  • Residual Unallocated Excess (Goodwill): $100,000

4. Sequence of Accounting Entries

To record the transaction and consolidate the balance sheets on the acquisition date, two separate journal entries are recognized:

Entry 1: Initial Acquisition Entry (Parent's Books)

Apex Holdings Inc. records the cash outflow and the acquisition of Beacon Technologies stock:
